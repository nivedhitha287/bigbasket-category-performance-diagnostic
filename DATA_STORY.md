# BigBasket Category Performance Data Story
## Executive Performance Diagnostic
Across the Jan–Jun 2026 diagnostic period, BigBasket generated **₹88,282.00** in total revenue across **434** delivered orders, yielding an overall Average Order Value (AOV) of **₹203.41**. Performance across the 6 product categories is divided evenly: **3 categories met performance targets**, while **3 categories fell short**.
---
## Category Performance Breakdown
### Above Target Tier (3 Categories Met)
* **Household Essentials:** Top revenue generator at **₹21,450.00** across 92 orders (AOV: ₹233.15). Exceeded the ₹15,000 target by **+₹6,450.00** (+43.0%).
* **Personal Care:** Second highest revenue at **₹16,800.00** across 74 orders (AOV: ₹227.03). Exceeded target by **+₹1,800.00** (+12.0%).
* **Bakery:** Generated **₹15,800.00** across 81 orders (AOV: ₹195.06). Exceeded target by **+₹800.00** (+5.3%).
### Below Target - Watch Tier
* **Dairy & Eggs:** Generated **₹14,200.00** across 78 orders (AOV: ₹182.05). Missed the ₹15,000 target by **-₹800.00** (-5.3%). High volume indicates stable demand, but lower basket value suppressed total revenue.
### Below Target - Critical Tier
* **Snacks & Beverages:** Generated **₹11,100.00** across 59 orders (AOV: ₹188.14). Missed target by **-₹3,900.00** (-26.0%).
* **Fruits & Vegetables:** Lowest revenue generator at **₹8,932.00** across 50 orders (AOV: ₹178.64). Missed target by **-₹6,068.00** (-40.5%).
---
## Data-Grounded Recommendations for Category Team

1. **Invest Marketing & Catalog Expansion in Household Essentials & Personal Care:**
   Household Essentials and Personal Care deliver the highest AOVs (₹233.15 and ₹227.03 respectively) and combined account for **43.3% of total revenue** (₹38,250 / ₹88,282). Allocating additional digital catalog visibility and promotional bundling to these high-value categories will maximize gross margin impact.

2. **Conduct Immediate Supplier & Sourcing Review for Fruits & Vegetables:**
   Fruits & Vegetables registered both the lowest order volume (50 orders) and the lowest AOV (₹178.64), missing its target revenue by **40.5%** (-₹6,068.00). The category team must review supplier pricing, stock availability, and freshness/fulfillment quality to address demand friction and recover basket conversion.
